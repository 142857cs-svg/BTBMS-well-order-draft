# Guide to the research notebooks

For the current exposition, read the [38-page English paper](../paper/paper.pdf)
and its [proof overview](../PROOF-OVERVIEW.md). The files in this directory are
the preserved Chinese research record from the original v139 upload package.
Their mathematical text has not been rewritten for the concise edition.

The names `current/` and `latest-argument.zh-CN.md` belong to the v139 snapshot.
They are retained so that existing links and section references remain valid.
The current article is in `../paper/`.

These notebooks retain progress reports, provisional assumptions and
superseded claims. Their section and theorem identifiers are preserved for
traceability. In particular, an old two-bare restriction is not a global
assumption of the current article. Consult the article for the final scope
and the [source concordance](../paper/SOURCE-CONCORDANCE.csv) for origins.

The notebooks are not required to compile the article. Historical references
to experiments do not imply that all those scripts or logs are included here.
The TypeScript files now included in `../paper/` pin the native definition;
they are not a packaged reproduction of the historical experiments.

See [SOURCE-NOTES.md](SOURCE-NOTES.md) for original formatting changes and
this edition's preservation checks.

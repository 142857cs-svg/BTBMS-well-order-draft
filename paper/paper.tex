\begin{abstract}
Let $\mathscr U$ be the finite expressions reached from the finite
branches of BTBMS Limit by its original fundamental sequences. Working
in $\mathsf{ZFC}+\text{``}0^\#\text{ exists''}$, we give a well-ordering
argument for the original recursive comparison on $\mathscr U$.
Finite completion, graded ancestor control, complete-window comparisons
and simultaneous Point construction yield a strict decrease from every
current legal labelling. A fixed finite-constructor-first order on $L$
provides canonical maps and certificates at the actual reflection levels,
including weak bare endpoints. Silver indiscernibles supply all finite
initial towers below one fixed bound. Least maximum labels, together
with a separate finite reachability argument, give an order-preserving
ordinal rank. The additional Limit vertex may be ranked by the third
Silver indiscernible.
\end{abstract}
\noindent\textit{Editorial note.}
This revision replaces the accumulated research appendices with the
general arguments used by the proof. It incorporates the three amendments

initial G approach and research constraints were supplied by the user;

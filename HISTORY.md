## Concise edition and package synchronization: 2026-10-26

The 38-page English paper is now the primary reading entry. The earlier
989-page consolidated edition reproduced the transitive closure of references
into the research notebooks. The concise edition replaces those historical
appendices with the general arguments used in the proof, retaining the native
operation appendix. The Chinese notebook files remain available at their
original paths for provenance.

The concise paper retains three preceding amendments: the required strength
and elementary relations of the actual work model; the entry-count argument
for an empty tail prefix; and source Gen for proper generated restrictions.
It shortens low graph-code coverage using source Gen for actual legal incoming
maps and independently established finite-term transport. It does not claim
that arbitrary weak cuts have canonical hull-collapse graphs. Thus the
edition's changes include explicit clarifications of arguments and scope,
not only page layout.

This GitHub package copies the concise article release unchanged into
`paper/`, supplies its build instructions, and updates the English navigation
and proof overview. It makes no additional mathematical changes. The Chinese
notebook Markdown and indices, the original source manifest and
`RELATED-WORK.md` are preserved byte for byte. 

The research status and attribution remain unchanged: substantive ChatGPT
assistance is acknowledged, the independent QQ proof is credited as reported,
and no independent human or proof-kernel verification is claimed. The 989-page
edition and review reports are not included as competing main articles in
this package.

## Separate proof and independence

The originator of F circulated a proof via QQ and acknowledged the independent
development of the G work, according to the repository author's account. The
exact circulation date and the date of that acknowledgement are not supplied
here. See [RELATED-WORK.md](RELATED-WORK.md).

This history does not infer an exact completion order from those statements.
It also does not treat the first mention of an approach as the completion of
a proof.

## Future updates

the relevant commit or release. If a revision uses a result, correction, or
idea from the QQ proof or another source, identify that dependence explicitly.

the distinction between an original document date, a later revision date, and
